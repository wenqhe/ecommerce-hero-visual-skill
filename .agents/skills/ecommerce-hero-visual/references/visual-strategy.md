# Visual Strategy Reference
视觉策略参考

## A. Product Hero Direction

Best for:
- clear product display
- strong ecommerce clarity
- high information efficiency

Features:
- large product
- clean background
- simple lighting
- strong headline area

## B. Lifestyle Direction

Best for:
- emotional appeal
- scenario-based communication
- daily-life relevance

Features:
- usage scene
- softer storytelling
- emotional atmosphere

## C. Feature-led Direction

Best for:
- functional differentiation
- technical or practical advantages

Features:
- product + simplified evidence graphics
- clearer benefit structure
- rational persuasion

## 4:5 Layout Suggestions

- product: roughly 35%–50% of frame
- headline: upper-left or upper-center
- price/CTA: lower-left or lower-right
- supporting points: compact
- reserve negative space for type

## Strategy Output Template

For each direction provide:
- direction name
- concept summary
- audience relevance
- scene/background
- composition
- color / light
- communication advantage
