# Visual Strategy Reference

Use these direction families as starting points, not fixed templates. Every proposed direction must be feasible with the approved reference assets.

## A. Product Hero Direction

Best for:

- clear product display;
- strong ecommerce clarity;
- high information efficiency;
- low fidelity risk when a clean PR image or approved cutout is available.

Typical features:

- large product;
- clean background;
- controlled lighting;
- strong headline area;
- direct side-by-side product comparison.

## B. Lifestyle Direction

Best for:

- emotional appeal;
- scenario-based communication;
- daily-life relevance.

Typical features:

- usage environment;
- softer storytelling;
- emotional atmosphere.

Reference caution:

- use only product views supported by PR assets;
- do not generate unseen wearable fit, hidden surfaces, grip, or interaction details;
- do not add unconfirmed accessories or usage outcomes;
- prefer direct product-layer composition when scene generation could change product identity.

## C. Feature-led Direction

Best for:

- functional differentiation;
- technical or practical advantages;
- evidence-led communication.

Typical features:

- product plus simplified evidence graphics;
- clear benefit structure;
- rational persuasion.

Reference caution:

- evidence graphics must be based on confirmed facts;
- PD assets may verify visible details but cannot create unsupported claims;
- do not use decorative technical graphics as proof of an unconfirmed function.

## Required Direction Evaluation

For every direction provide:

- direction name and concept summary;
- audience relevance;
- scene and background;
- composition and negative-space plan;
- color and lighting proposal;
- required product view;
- supporting PR, PD, BA, SR, and LR Reference IDs;
- Reference Feasibility: Supported, Conditional, or Blocked;
- occlusion and unknown-detail risk;
- Fidelity Risk: Low, Medium, High, or Blocked;
- intended product production method;
- communication advantage;
- mitigation for every Conditional or High-risk item.

Do not recommend a Blocked direction. Prefer the lowest-risk direction that still meets the communication goal.

## Layout Reference Rules

Layout Reference may inform only:

- composition;
- information density;
- visual hierarchy;
- text/product balance;
- negative-space planning.

Layout Reference must not influence:

- product shape;
- product color;
- product material;
- product logo;
- product features;
- product accessories;
- product facts.

Do not reproduce the reference design, distinctive arrangement, branded graphics, copy, or proprietary expression. Convert it into general layout principles and adapt those principles to the current product, canvas, copy, and Brand Assets.

## 4:5 Layout Suggestions

Use only when compatible with the approved references and task:

- product: roughly 35%–50% of frame;
- headline: upper-left or upper-center;
- price and CTA: lower-left or lower-right;
- supporting points: compact;
- reserve negative space for type;
- avoid cropping a locked product feature or required QA area.
